  • Gulmarg – Snow-Capped Peaks and Adventure Awaits

    Experience the thrill of Gulmarg, where you can ski on powdery slopes, enjoy breathtaking views from the Gondola, or simply walk through its beautiful meadows.

  • Sonamarg – The Valley of Gold

    With its towering peaks and pristine rivers, Sonamarg is a nature lover's dream. Trek, camp, or just relax in this tranquil paradise.

  • Pahalgam – The Peaceful Escape

    Known for its lush greenery, serene rivers, and peaceful atmosphere, Pahalgam offers a perfect mix of relaxation and outdoor exploration.

  • Srinagar – The Heart of Kashmir

    From the serene Dal Lake to the magnificent Mughal Gardens, Srinagar is the heart of Kashmir. Enjoy a Shikara ride on Dal Lake, explore the historical sites like the Shankaracharya Temple, and indulge in authentic Kashmiri cuisine. Srinagar is the perfect blend of culture and natural beauty.

  • Doodhpathri – The Milk Valley

    Escape to Doodhpathri, a serene valley with vast meadows, flowing streams, and breathtaking views—a true hidden gem in Kashmir.

  • Yousmarg – Your Tranquil Retreat

    Yousmarg is a quiet paradise surrounded by dense forests and majestic mountains. It's perfect for those seeking solitude, natural beauty, and peace.

  • Gurez Valley – Your Offbeat Kashmir Destination

    Nestled in the heart of Kashmir, Gurez Valley is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. With its breathtaking landscapes, pristine rivers, and snow-capped peaks, Gurez Valley offers a serene escape into nature's untouched beauty.

  • Bangus Valley – The Valley From Fairy Tales

    Bangus Valley, often referred to as Kashmir's best-kept secret, is a paradise for nature lovers. The valley's lush green meadows, vibrant flora, and crystal-clear streams create a picturesque haven that feels like a dream come true.

  • Vaishno Devi – Spiritual Journey Awaits

    The Vaishno Devi Yatra is one of the most revered pilgrimages in India, attracting millions of devotees each year. Nestled in the Trikuta Mountains, the holy shrine of Vaishno Devi is a symbol of faith and devotion.
