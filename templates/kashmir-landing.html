Popular Destinations of Kashmir

Explore the breathtaking landscapes that make Kashmir paradise on earth

Gulmarg – Snow-Capped Peaks

Experience the thrill of Gulmarg, where you can ski on powdery slopes, enjoy breathtaking views from the Gondola, or simply walk through its beautiful meadows.

Sonamarg – The Valley of Gold

With its towering peaks and pristine rivers, Sonamarg is a nature lover's dream. Trek, camp, or just relax in this tranquil paradise.

Pahalgam – The Peaceful Escape

Known for its lush greenery, serene rivers, and peaceful atmosphere, Pahalgam offers a perfect mix of relaxation and outdoor exploration.

Srinagar – Heart of Kashmir

From the serene Dal Lake to the magnificent Mughal Gardens. Enjoy a Shikara ride, explore Shankaracharya Temple, and indulge in authentic cuisine.

Doodhpathri – The Milk Valley

Escape to Doodhpathri, a serene valley with vast meadows, flowing streams, and breathtaking views—a true hidden gem in Kashmir.

Yousmarg – Your Tranquil Retreat

A quiet paradise surrounded by dense forests and majestic mountains. Perfect for those seeking solitude, natural beauty, and peace.

Gurez – Offbeat Destination

Nestled in the heart of Kashmir, Gurez is a hidden gem. With pristine rivers and snow-capped peaks, it offers a serene escape into untouched beauty.

Bangus – Valley of Fairy Tales

Kashmir's best-kept secret. Lush green meadows, vibrant flora, and crystal-clear streams create a picturesque haven that feels like a dream.

Vaishno Devi – Spiritual Journey

One of the most revered pilgrimages in India. Nestled in the Trikuta Mountains, this holy shrine is a symbol of faith and devotion.

Tulail Valley – The Hidden Realm

A remote and pristine valley beyond Gurez, offering untouched natural beauty, traditional wooden houses, and a glimpse into a simpler way of life.

Tosa Maidan – The King's Meadow

A vast, historic meadow surrounded by dense forests and snow-capped peaks. Perfect for trekking, camping, and experiencing panoramic natural grandeur.

Warwan Valley – Rugged & Remote

For the true adventurer, Warwan offers isolation, stunning waterfalls, and rugged landscapes. A challenging yet rewarding destination for trekkers.

Keran Valley – On the Edge of Beauty

Located on the banks of the Kishanganga River along the LoC, Keran boasts lush green forests, charming wooden houses, and serene riverside views.

Sinthan Top – The Mountain Pass

A high-altitude pass offering breathtaking 360-degree views of both Kashmir and Jammu divisions. Famous for its year-round snow and scenic drive.

Lolab Valley – The Land of Love

A beautiful oval-shaped valley known for its dense forests, natural springs, and warm hospitality. A perfect spot for a peaceful and authentic Kashmiri experience.
